This module requires users to accept the legal terms before paying
through the payment form. It also stores the acceptance metadata in the
payment transaction.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

1. Create a quotation.

2. Access the payment form using either of these methods:

   - Generate and open a payment link from the quotation.
   - Send the quotation to the customer and access it from the portal to
     sign and pay it.

3. Select a payment method.

4. Accept the legal terms.

5. Proceed with the payment.
