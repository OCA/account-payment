#. Go to *Invoicing > Customers > Invoices* or *Invoicing > Vendors > Bills*.
#. Select one or more documents carrying an analytic distribution and click on
   *Register Payment*.
#. Confirm the wizard.
#. Open the resulting payment and go to the *Journal Items* tab: the
   receivable/payable line carries the analytic distribution of the documents
   that were paid.

The distribution is computed when the payment is created. Changing the
analytic distribution of an invoice afterwards does not update the payments
already registered, and a distribution set by hand on the payment is kept.
