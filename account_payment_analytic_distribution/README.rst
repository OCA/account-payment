When a payment is registered from one or more invoices, this module copies the
analytic distribution of the paid documents onto the counterpart journal item
of the payment.

The distribution is weighted by the amount of each journal item, so an invoice
split 90/10 between two analytic accounts produces a payment split 90/10 as
well. Amounts carrying no analytic account on the invoice stay unallocated on
the payment.

Analytic reporting on a cash basis becomes possible this way: the analytic
items generated when the payment is posted carry the same analytic accounts,
in the same proportion, as the ones generated by the invoice.

Usage
=====

#. Go to *Invoicing > Customers > Invoices* or *Invoicing > Vendors > Bills*.
#. Select one or more documents carrying an analytic distribution and click on
   *Register Payment*.
#. Confirm the wizard.
#. Open the resulting payment and go to the *Journal Items* tab: the
   receivable/payable line carries the analytic distribution of the documents
   that were paid.

The distribution is computed when the payment is created. Changing the
analytic distribution of an invoice afterwards does not update the payments
already registered, and a distribution set by hand on the payment is kept.
