This module extends the **Generate a Payment Link**
(``payment.link.wizard``) wizard on customer invoices (``account.move``)
to make the payment currency (``currency_id``) selectable and
automatically convert amounts when switching currencies.

Key features:

- Makes the **Currency** (``currency_id``) field visible and editable in
  the payment link wizard.
- Automatically converts the remaining amount due (``amount_max``), the
  payment amount (``amount``), and open installment previews using the
  invoice's exact exchange rate when switching between the invoice
  currency and the company currency (or the date's exchange rate for
  other currencies).
- Generates a signed ``/payment/pay`` link in the selected currency when
  it differs from the invoice currency (e.g., generating a ``TRY``
  payment link for a ``USD`` invoice when the payment provider POS only
  supports ``TRY``).
- Automatically links incoming transactions from ``/payment/pay`` back
  to the corresponding posted invoice by reference so payment creation
  and cross-currency reconciliation happen automatically upon payment
  completion.

Usage
=====

To generate a payment link in a different currency than the invoice
currency:

1. Go to *Invoicing > Customers > Invoices* and open a posted invoice
   (for example, an invoice in ``USD``).
2. Click the gear icon (*Action*) and select *Generate a Payment Link*.
3. Change the *Currency* field (for example, from ``USD`` to ``TRY``).
4. The *Amount* and *Amount Due* fields are automatically converted to
   the selected currency using the invoice exchange rate. You can also
   adjust the *Amount* to collect a partial payment.
5. Click *Generate and Copy Payment Link* and share the link with the
   customer.
6. When the customer completes the payment, Odoo creates the payment in
   the selected currency and reconciles it against the invoice
   automatically.
