To generate a payment link in a different currency than the invoice currency:

1. Go to *Invoicing > Customers > Invoices* and open a posted invoice (for
   example, an invoice in `USD`).
2. Click the gear icon (*Action*) and select *Generate a Payment Link*.
3. Change the *Currency* field (for example, from `USD` to `TRY`).
4. The *Amount* and *Amount Due* fields are automatically converted to the
   selected currency using the invoice exchange rate. You can also adjust the
   *Amount* to collect a partial payment.
5. Click *Generate and Copy Payment Link* and share the link with the customer.
6. When the customer completes the payment, Odoo creates the payment in the
   selected currency and reconciles it against the invoice automatically.
